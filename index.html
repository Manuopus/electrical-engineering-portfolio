About me

I am interested in how electrical energy is turned into something useful: light, motion, measurement, information. I want to understand circuits well enough to design them, not only to copy them.

My plan is a dual study programme in Electrical Engineering (Elektrotechnik) in Germany, where theory at university and practice in a company run side by side. That is also how this portfolio works: each project starts with the theory and ends with a real result.

Everything here is honest about its state. Planned work is marked as planned, and a skill only counts as demonstrated once a project on this page proves it.
